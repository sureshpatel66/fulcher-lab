Research program

From mucosal biology to clinically relevant discovery

Our work examines how local immune environments, microbial communities, and external exposures shape HIV susceptibility, inflammation, and health outcomes.

01

HIV and mucosal immunity

Investigating biological processes at mucosal surfaces that influence HIV acquisition, transmission, and immune activation.

02

Microbiome–host interactions

Studying oral and gut microbial communities alongside inflammatory and clinical measures in HIV.

03

Translational research

Connecting clinical samples, laboratory methods, and rigorous computational analysis to actionable questions in infectious diseases.
