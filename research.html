Research

Biology at the mucosal interface

We study how immune responses, microbes, and clinical exposures interact in HIV and related infectious-disease contexts.

Theme 01

HIV transmission and susceptibility

We investigate factors that shape vulnerability to HIV infection, with attention to tissue-level inflammation and the biological context of exposure.

Related publication →

Theme 02

Mucosal immune environments

We examine local cytokine and immune signals that may influence HIV risk and disease processes at rectal and gastrointestinal mucosal sites.

Related publication →

Theme 03

Microbiome and inflammation

We connect microbial ecology with systemic inflammation and clinical outcomes, using molecular, immunologic, and computational approaches.

Related publication →

Approach

Translational and collaborative by design

  • Clinical infectious-diseases research
  • Mucosal sampling and biomarker analysis
  • Microbiome and genomics-informed investigation
  • Reproducible computational analysis

Research context

Substance use and HIV

Published work from the group has examined associations between recent methamphetamine use and rectal mucosal inflammatory cytokines, a clinically important context for HIV prevention and care.
